Security, Privacy and Safety.

Security, privacy and safety are at the core of Happy to Help, because we understand there’s no room for compromise when it comes to trust and data protection. We recognize the sensitivity of monitoring user activity with advanced LLMs, which is why we’ve designed our platform to ensure your user's data remains private, encrypted, and under your control at every step. Your trust is our responsibility.

✦

Client-Side Encryption.

Raw activity data is encrypted with keys which persist only in each user's browser. This ensures even our system can’t access personal activity data when the user is not connected.

✦

Your Users, Your Control.

User privacy is our top priority. You have full control over what data collected and how it’s used. You can choose which users to monitor and which LLMs analyze activity.

✦

Human Oversight.

We keep humans in the loop. While we minimize intervention, oversight counts. When AI isn’t enough, users can seamlessly connect with a human, always ensuring exceptional support.
