Our values.

This is what drives us.

The future of AI may be highly disruptive, creating as many challenges as opportunities. But at Happy to Help, we see a world where AI is a force for good, a tool that empowers everyone to achieve more with less. By giving this power to everyone, not just the largest or most tech-savvy business, we’re democratizing AI and making it accessible to all. Our platform is designed to harness the full potential of AI, enabling you to focus on what matters most: your customers, your products, and your own vision.

We’ve built Happy to Help around a set of core values that guide our every decision and innovation. These values are more than just words—they’re the bedrock of our platform, the foundation of our culture, and the promise we make to every business that entrusts us with their future.

Safety

Every decision we make is made with a view to its long-term impact, ensuring our technology is a force for good.

Security

We believe trust is earned by safeguarding every piece of data. Every interaction must be protected and secure by default.

Privacy

We empower users with control over their data, ensuring they know exactly who has access and how it’s used.

Empathy

We strive to understand our users’ perspectives and challenges, designing solutions that truly make a difference in their experiences.

Equity

True success is shared, not hoarded by a privileged few. Opportunities should flow to everyone for a better future for all.

Respect

We believe diverse perspectives make us better, fostering an inclusive environment where each individual is valued.

Integrity

We operate with honesty and authenticity, maintaining trust in all interactions—within our team and with our customers.

Vision

We build for the future, considering the big picture so our solutions benefit not just a few, but many—and for years to come.
